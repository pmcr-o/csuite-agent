# Executive Council

Route: `@csuite-agent /csuite:executive-council`.

Fan out only to registered C-suite child agents. Preserve each child's local trail and evidence status. The council may synthesize tradeoffs and delegation proposals, but it cannot manufacture consensus, self-certify a decision, or execute external side effects.
